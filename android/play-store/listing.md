**Full description** (max 4000):

SpatialEQ shapes the sound of your phone. Place the sound around your head in 3D, fine-tune it with a precise parametric equalizer, and hear the result in every app that allows it.

🎧 VIRTUAL SURROUND FOR HEADPHONES
• Stereo, virtual 5.1 or virtual 7.1 binaural rendering
• Drag each virtual speaker around your head in the live 3D view
• Adjustable room size and reverb for anything from a small studio to a concert hall

🎚 PARAMETRIC EQUALIZER
• Up to 16 bands: peak, low/high shelf, low/high pass
• Drag the handles on the graph or set the exact frequency, gain and Q
• Import headphone correction profiles in AutoEq / Equalizer APO format
• Live spectrum that moves with your music

🔊 SPEAKER MODE
• Stereo widening and crosstalk cancellation for a wider sound from your phone's speakers

✨ ENHANCE
• Bass boost with harmonic enhancement for small speakers and earbuds
• Dialogue boost for clearer voices in videos and podcasts
• Volume leveler that evens out loud and quiet content
• Look-ahead limiter that prevents distortion

📱 YOUR APPS, YOUR CHOICE
• Works with music, video and game apps that allow audio capture
• Switch individual apps on or off on the Apps screen
• 12 presets (Cinema, Music Hall, Gaming, Podcast, Bass Boost and more), plus your own
• Remembers a separate sound for your headphones, earbuds and phone speaker

HOW IT WORKS
SpatialEQ uses Android's audio playback capture to process other apps' sound on your device. Tap Start and confirm Android's screen-capture prompt; SpatialEQ captures only app audio, never your screen or microphone. Your audio is never recorded or sent anywhere.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Requires Android 10 or later.
• Some apps block audio capture, so their sound stays unprocessed. Examples include some streaming services and browsers.
• For the most complete coverage, grant one optional permission from a computer:
  adb shell pm grant com.savannahdsp.spatialeq android.permission.DUMP
• Processing adds a small amount of audio delay.
