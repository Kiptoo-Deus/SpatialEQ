SpatialEQ Privacy Policy

Savannah DSP · Effective 9 October 2026 · Applies to SpatialEQ for Android, iOS and macOS

SpatialEQ is an equalizer and spatial audio app. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: your audio is processed on your device and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The free Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below.

Audio

  • SpatialEQ processes sound in real time on your device to apply equalization and spatial effects.
  • Audio is never saved, recorded to a file, uploaded or shared. It exists only briefly in memory while being processed.
  • On Android, SpatialEQ uses Android's audio playback capture to process other apps' sound. Android requires the Record audio permission and a screen-capture confirmation for this. SpatialEQ captures only app audio (media and games). It never records the microphone and never captures what is on your screen.
  • On macOS, SpatialEQ uses the system audio recording permission for the same purpose and with the same limits.

Information stored on your device

  • Your settings, presets, per-device sound profiles and the list of apps you chose to process are stored locally on your device.
  • On iOS, music files you import stay in the app's storage on your device.
  • SpatialEQ has no accounts and does not send this information to us.

Permissions (Android)

  • Record audio and screen capture confirmation: required by Android to process other apps' sound, as described above.
  • Notifications: shows that processing is running, with a Stop button.
  • App list (launchable apps): shows your apps on the Apps screen so you can choose which ones are processed. This list stays on your device.
  • Optional system diagnostic access (granted manually over ADB): lets SpatialEQ see which apps are currently playing audio so it can process them. This information is used only on your device.
  • Head tracking (iOS and macOS): motion data from supported headphones keeps virtual sound sources in place. It is used only on your device.

Advertising (Android)

The Android app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, SpatialEQ asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Presets → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

SpatialEQ is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
